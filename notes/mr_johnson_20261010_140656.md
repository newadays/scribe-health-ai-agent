He mentions getting about five hours of sleep per night and attributes his fatigue to work-related stress. He takes ibuprofen three to four times a week for headache relief.

**Objective:**  
- Blood Pressure: 148/92 mmHg  
- Pulse: 78 bpm

- Weight: 94 kg  
- BMI: 29.7 (calculated)  

**Assessment:**  
1. Headaches, likely tension-type, possibly related to stress and inadequate sleep.  
2. Fatigue, likely secondary to poor sleep quality and stress.  
3. Elevated blood pressure, stage 1 hypertension.

**Plan:**  
- Recheck blood pressure in 2 weeks.  
- Advise patient to cut back on ibuprofen use.  
- Encourage aiming for 7 hours of sleep per night.  
- Order basic metabolic panel and lipid panel.  
- If blood pressure remains elevated at follow-up, consider starting lisinopril.
