**Subjective:**
- Patient is a 6-year-old female presenting with fever and ear pain. 
- Mother reports fever since last night, peaking at 38.9°C. Acetaminophen (children's Tylenol) was given around 10 PM. 
- No vomiting, rash, or trouble breathing noted. Patient has been fussy and not eating much. 

**Objective:**
- Weight: 21 kg
- Temperature: 38.4°C
- Right eardrum: red and bulging; left ear normal. Throat slightly red. Lungs clear.

**Assessment:**
- Right-sided acute otitis media.

**Plan:**
- Prescribe amoxicillin at 945 mg total daily dose (45 mg/kg/day) split into two doses for 10 days. 
- Continue acetaminophen for fever and pain management. 
- Advise to call if fever persists beyond 48 hours on antibiotics or if symptoms worsen. 
- Immunizations are up to date; no additional vaccinations needed at this time.
